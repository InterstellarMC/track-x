Eight ideal cylinders span radii 0.04–0.32 m. Tracks start on the beam axis. For B along +z and outward motion the signed curvature k = −0.299792458 qB/pT (1/m, T, GeV/c). At radius r the azimuth advances by asin(kr/2), and z advances by transverse arc length times pz/pT. Gaussian tangential and longitudinal measurement errors, Bernoulli hit losses and uniform clutter approximate readout. Independent longitudinal vertices model vertex spread. Material, field maps, secondaries and curling returns are omitted. Higher occupancy creates competing links among tracks. ACTS also models propagation through material and transports parameter covariance. See [ACTS tracking](https://acts.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tracking.html).
