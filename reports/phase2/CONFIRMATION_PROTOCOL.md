# Frozen confirmation protocol

Following the exploratory results on events 1003–1005, freeze the directional builder, 32 sectors, geometry-only hypothesis ranking, GNN-weighted ranking and global ownership resolver. Use the next three in-order archive IDs 1006, 1007 and 1008 with all hit/truth/cell/particle tables. Read the same detectors.csv and frozen local_gnn weights from the first sample. No training, threshold calibration, event exclusion or checkpoint selection uses these three additional events.

For every event compare geometry-only one pass versus two passes (offsets zero and π/32). Also compare geometry-only with GNN-weighted two-pass candidates. Both use identical final geometry-based ownership resolution. Primary endpoints are official full-event score and volume-8 matching efficiency. Secondary endpoints are fake and duplicate rates, purity, completeness and latency. Record each event, paired deltas and the mean; three events do not support a broad significance claim. Test the directional hypotheses that two passes improve official score and efficiency. Estimate the GNN-weight difference without choosing the winner on the confirmation sample. Preserve the exploratory three-event results separately.
