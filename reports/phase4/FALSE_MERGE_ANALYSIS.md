Mixed requires two signal IDs. Severe requires two hits apiece from two signal IDs. Single background requires exactly one noise hit and only one signal ID. Multiple wrong counts paths with two or more hits outside their largest signal contribution. Transition switch compares the last seed and first extension hit and requires distinct nonzero IDs. These path fractions overlap. Fragmentation is the fraction of eligible particles contributing at least two hits to two or more paths. Duplicate counts repeat qualifying matches divided by all paths. Final assignments are exclusive. The measurements do not establish that splitting a mixed seed recovers either trajectory. No track-splitting operation was selected or tested.
