# Benchmark definitions and result tables

The [ten-event no-gap comparison](NO_GAP_CONFIRMATION.md), [development ablations](ABLATION_STUDY.md), [final event scores and paired estimates](CONFIRMATION_RESULTS.md) and [interactive figures](measurements.html) are generated from the event JSON records. All score and efficiency denominators cover the complete simulated event. Reconstruction uses only ten barrel layers and prompt outward seeds.

* Official score sums the normalized weights of correct overlaps satisfying both strict majorities in the reference implementation. Unassigned hits are included in the complete submission under the existing Phase III convention. [Pinned scorer](https://github.com/LAL/trackml-library/blob/53a165e15a2c885f54c2bef1bd1ed53db6ed9648/trackml/score.py)
* Custom eligible particles have ≥3 full-event hits. A candidate matches if more than half its assigned hits belong to the particle and at least half the particle's full hit set is present. First qualifying match in seed order counts once. Efficiency divides unique matches by eligible particles. Duplicate and fake fractions divide repeat matches and remaining unmatched candidates respectively by all candidates.
* Purity and completeness average the respective candidate and particle overlap fractions over unique matches. The length plot uses every path and divides the largest signal contribution by path length before averaging. The pT figures use the generator particle momentum in GeV and count unique matches within each truth bin.
* Coverage is assigned hits divided by all hits. Assigned weight is the fraction of total truth weight on any assigned hit; official score measures correctly recovered weight under challenge matching. Per-volume matched-hit efficiency divides true hits in full-event matched paths in that volume by all its signal hits.
* Mixed, severe, wrong-hit, fragmentation, switch and conflict definitions are in [failure analysis](FALSE_MERGE_ANALYSIS.md). Candidate recall divides offered majority-signal seed/surface associations by all seed/surface pairs with a corresponding truth hit, including terminated seeds. Curvature error in the shared benchmark compares a common robust prompt refit against endpoint truth bending when both endpoints belong to the same signal particle. The independent EKF assessment uses its posterior directly. [Covariance validation](COVARIANCE_VALIDATION.md)
* Graph, seeding, evaluation and reconstruction seconds are measured per event. Recorded process high-water memory includes evaluation. Statistical comparisons give every event equal weight. No threshold, feature or method was changed in response to final scores. [Supported truth weight and regional efficiency](DETECTOR_COVERAGE.md)
